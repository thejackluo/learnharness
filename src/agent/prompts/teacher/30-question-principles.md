# Question principles — what a teach-phase check must be

These govern `type: "check"` questions (and explain). Predictions have their own doctrine — the predict/check cycle file — and deliberately violate the first two principles: they are about the untaught next step, and guessing IS the point.

Every check exists to answer one question: did the idea actually **take** — not whether he can read it back. The principles:

- **Answerable by reasoning from the session.** Every check must be answerable by reasoning from what the session has covered — never require anything he couldn't have gotten from the lesson itself. A check he has no route to is not a check, it's a coin flip.
- **Never just reciting your words back.** A check that only asks him to parrot the sentence just above it is copywork, not evidence.
- **Prefer problems that apply the knowledge.** The strongest evidence that an idea took is seeing it USED — applied to a new case, used to predict something, extended somewhere it hasn't been. Reach for applied questions first; when the idea genuinely can't be applied, an honest recall check is fine — but it's the fallback, not the habit.
- **Catch confusion early, and make it nameable.** Design checks so a miss TELLS you which wrong model he holds — in a multiple-choice check each wrong option is the answer he’d give under a specific wrong model; in a free-recall check his own words reveal it. Diagnose and repair before building anything on top of it.
- **His answer is signal, not verdict.** A lucky right answer is nothing; a wrong answer on an honest check is gold — confusion caught early is cheap to fix.

### Pick the format by subject

Use a typed part when the subject has an answer the app can verify exactly or numerically; keep reasoning, derivations, and explanations in free or structured criteria. Numeric units, tolerances, accepted strings, field labels, and criteria are committed before the question. The app grades numeric and exact parts; use `evaluate` only for free and structured criteria.

- **Physics — numeric result plus derivation:** `{"type":"check","nodeId":"kinematics","question":"Find the speed and show the derivation.","correctAnswer":"Speed is distance divided by elapsed time; the result is 12.5 m/s.","parts":[{"id":"speed","kind":"numeric","prompt":"Find the speed.","answer":12.5,"units":[{"symbol":"m/s","factor":1},{"symbol":"km/h","factor":0.2777777778}],"tolerance":{"kind":"absolute","value":0.1},"points":2},{"id":"derivation","prompt":"Show the derivation.","criteria":[{"id":"equation","description":"Uses the speed relation","maxPoints":2,"expectedEvidence":"Distance divided by elapsed time."}]}]}`
- **Math — exact value plus worked steps:** `{"type":"check","nodeId":"fractions","question":"Reduce one half and show the key steps.","correctAnswer":"The reduced fraction is 1/2.","parts":[{"id":"fraction","kind":"exact","prompt":"Reduce the fraction.","accepted":["1/2","0.5"],"normalize":"nfkc-trim","points":1},{"id":"steps","prompt":"Show the key algebraic steps.","criteria":[{"id":"work","description":"Valid simplification","maxPoints":2,"expectedEvidence":"Numerator and denominator are divided by the same factor."}]}]}`
- **Philosophy — structured argument, objection, and reply:** `{"type":"check","nodeId":"argument-validity","question":"Reconstruct the argument, then test it with an objection and reply.","correctAnswer":"State both premises and the conclusion; the reply should address the objection's strongest point.","parts":[{"id":"argument","kind":"structured","prompt":"Reconstruct and test the argument.","fields":[{"id":"premise-1","label":"Premise 1","kind":"line"},{"id":"premise-2","label":"Premise 2","kind":"line"},{"id":"conclusion","label":"Conclusion","kind":"line"},{"id":"objection","label":"Objection","kind":"paragraph"},{"id":"reply","label":"Reply","kind":"paragraph"}],"criteria":[{"id":"structure","description":"Reconstructs the inference","maxPoints":2,"expectedEvidence":"The conclusion follows from the stated premises."},{"id":"reply","description":"Answers the objection","maxPoints":2,"expectedEvidence":"The reply addresses the objection's strongest point."}]}]}`
- **Languages — exact recall plus translation:** `{"type":"check","nodeId":"vocabulary","question":"Recall the word, then translate the sentence.","correctAnswer":"The spoken word is ねこ, meaning cat; the sentence preserves its stated subject and action.","parts":[{"id":"kana","kind":"exact","prompt":"Write the kana for the spoken word.","accepted":["ねこ"],"normalize":"nfkc-trim","points":1},{"id":"translation","prompt":"Translate the sentence and explain its grammar.","criteria":[{"id":"meaning","description":"Preserves the meaning","maxPoints":2,"expectedEvidence":"The translation retains the subject and action."}]}]}`
- **CS/OS — trace and debug:** `{"type":"check","nodeId":"request-tracing","question":"Trace the request, locate the fault, and propose a repair.","correctAnswer":"Follow the state through each step and change the faulty behavior without affecting unrelated paths.","parts":[{"id":"trace","kind":"structured","prompt":"Trace the request through the service.","fields":[{"id":"state","label":"State after the request","kind":"paragraph"},{"id":"fault","label":"Fault location","kind":"line"},{"id":"repair","label":"Repair","kind":"paragraph"}],"criteria":[{"id":"trace-state","description":"Tracks the state correctly","maxPoints":2,"expectedEvidence":"The state change is followed through each relevant step."},{"id":"repair","description":"Identifies a safe fix","maxPoints":2,"expectedEvidence":"The repair addresses the cause without changing unrelated behavior."}]}]}`
